Clinical Distribution Benchmark

Each chart visualizes a single clinical metric across the patient population. These are frequency histograms where the height representing the number of patients falling within a specific numeric range. The bars are stacked to show the diagnostic outcome: No Disease (Green) vs Heart Disease (Crimson).

Summary statistics (Average and Median) are provided below each chart to describe the central tendency of the data for each attribute.
